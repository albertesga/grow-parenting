§ 00 — Intent

What this document is, and what it isn't.

Session 1 of an 8-session build. We start at the foundation: the principles, the palette, the type, the avatar mapping. No components yet — those are Session 3. This is the air the rest of the system will breathe in.

The brief, distilled

Grow is a quiet companion for the first 1,000 days. It is not a tracking optimizer. It is not a celebratory app. It is not a cold medical interface.

It is a magazine of care. The visual language that follows is editorial: generous whitespace, soft cream paper, a single ink, restrained color. Color earns its place.

What changes from v1 wireframes

The symbolic ●/⬤ avatar system is retired. The three soft 3D characters become the canon. They live small or medium — never as full-screen mascots.

Color leaves the structure and lives only in the avatars and rare accents. The structure is paper, ink, line.

§ 01 — Principles

Five rules that hold the system together.

When in doubt during any later session, return to these. They are non-negotiable.
P · 01

Air is the protagonist.

If you're choosing between less whitespace and more, choose more. A section breathes or it shouldn't exist.

P · 02

Hierarchy by size and weight, never by hue.

Color almost never carries meaning. Ink and paper do most of the work. Brand colors appear with an eyedropper, not a brush.

P · 03

The avatar is a character, not a mascot.

Cute but adult. The smile is the whole emotion. No tears, no eyebrows, no exclamation marks. Feeling lives in the context, not on the face.

P · 04

Trauma-informed before everything.

No red alarms. No medical-thriller language. Attention states use border, icon, and word — color is the last resort.

P · 05

Editorial cadence, not product chrome.

Short sentences. One idea per line. The occasional sticker, pulled-quote, or hand underline. Quiet asymmetry instead of rigid grids.

P · 06

Made by humans, for humans.

If a sentence sounds like a robot wrote it, it gets rewritten. If a screen feels like a SaaS funnel, it gets re-laid.

§ 10 — Decisions

Three things I need from you before Session 02.

If you say "go" without commenting, I'll proceed with the defaults shown here.
Decision · 01 · Avatar mapping

Confirm Coral = embarazo, Blush = bebé, Mint = crianza.

Default proceed if no comment. Alternative on offer: swap Mint and Coral (Mint = embarazo, calmer). I lean toward the current mapping because the mint character carries the wordmark and reads as the "spokes-character".

Decision · 02 · Palette intensity

Are these brand bases warm enough — or should Coral push 5–8% more saturated?

The eyedropped values are honest to the 3D references. They will look paler at small sizes (xs 36 / sm 60). I can pre-bake a "small-avatar tint" that nudges chroma up ~6% so the avatars don't disappear in list rows.

Decision · 03 · Italic discipline

Italic Lenia = manifesto + clinical citations only. Confirm.

This means UI labels never go italic, body copy never goes italic, and pull-quotes use italic only when they originate from a cited source. Stricter than usual editorial practice — but it protects the manifesto's weight.
